# 11 — Role-specific learning and hypothetical scoring

**Not a delivered programme.** NIST SP 800-50 Rev 1 emphasizes a lifecycle learning programme with measurement of behavior, not an assumption that presentation attendance guarantees learning.

Our [feedback mapping](../data/learning_feedback_map.csv) connects each of the eight original knowledge items with a correct option, safety rationale and coaching prompt. A separate [rollout plan](../data/awareness_rollout_plan.csv) records accessibility and privacy safeguards that require approval.

## Worked answer sheets — no participants

Three **synthetic** personas illustrate validation of a scoring algorithm: MODEL-CAUTIOUS: 8/8, MODEL-MIXED: 6/8, MODEL-NEEDS-COACHING: 3/8. These figures are constructed from an answer key, are **not employee test outcomes**, and must not be used as pass rate, training effectiveness or actual awareness improvement statistics.

The planned 7/8 threshold is illustrative. Any real learning evaluation would require appropriate population sampling, role-specific assessment, voluntary/authorized exercises, accessible delivery, data minimization and safeguards against adverse personnel use of raw scores. Future outcome measures should assess reliable reporting, safe access approvals and correct escalation behaviors, not just recall.

## No fabricated improvement

Original `data/learning_metrics.csv` remains entirely `NOT MEASURED` with blank values. Neither mock learner scoring nor writing a tabletop script changes an actual organizational control or proves risk reduction.
