## Safe future training boundaries

- Never launch a phishing email or vendor impersonation outside an expressly approved exercise.
- Do not collect live passwords, MFA recovery codes or real customer records; use inert materials.
- Use privacy-preserving, aggregated and voluntary/authorized learning measurement as applicable.
- Make materials accessible to staff with varied abilities and language needs.
- Keep manager approval, HR participation and incident response authority distinct.
- Escalate privacy notification decisions to appropriate specialists; technical ticket reads alone do not confirm exfiltration or reportability.
