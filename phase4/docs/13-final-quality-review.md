## What is not evidenced

This educational project does **not** establish that a threat actor compromised the support identity, that customer information was exported, that credentials were stolen, or that any employee completed the awareness modules. A record of 41 reads is not proof of data exfiltration. The scenarios dated after 5 November 2026 are deliberately hypothetical future events.

It contains no actual exercise observation sheet, genuine employee quiz result, authentic incident ticket, vendor authorization, signed executive response, privacy/legal breach decision, independent containment test or confirmed business recovery. The scoring program tests only its own constructed models, not risk reduction.
