# 09 — Branch-based tabletop facilitator and scoring guide

**MODEL SOLUTIONS ONLY — NO REAL EXERCISE TOOK PLACE.** Based on the fictional scenario starting 18 November 2026.

## Decision approach

For each original inject (`INJ-01` to `INJ-06`), read the cue, available evidence IDs and role assignments before revealing the two prepared choices in [the branch register](../data/decision_branches.csv). Participants *in a future authorized delivery* should state the working hypothesis, what is and is not proven, the authority to approve action and an observable verification method. This teaching file has **not been used with participants**.

## Model scoring

- **2 points:** evidence-limited safe decision, accountable owner and appropriate decision/verification gate.
- **1 point:** direction plausible but missing authorization, evidence or test of effectiveness.
- **0 points:** unsupported conclusion, unsafe irreversible step, missing authority or fictitious closure.

Six injects produce a **maximum 12 points**. An example threshold of **10/12 with no unsafe 0 on the recovery/privacy gates** is for future *training evaluation only*, not an incident-response certification. The two prepared branches per inject score 2 or 0; partial scores apply to free-form participant answers only in a future delivered exercise.

The [fictional model answers](../data/illustrative_tabletop_answers.csv) provide a worked evidence-led profile (12/12) and rushed profile (2/12). Neither score represents any actual person, team, employer, or training effectiveness result. The data are intentionally designed to illustrate how incorrect decisions accumulate risk.

## Exercise safeguards

Before any real delivery: obtain written exercise authorization, confirm facilitator independence, avoid production changes, avoid actual phishing emails and capture consent/retention decisions. Keep the privacy lead's notification assessment independent from technical facts; never infer disclosure merely from ticket reads. Recovery must be blocked until identity-session scope, access rollback and service-owner acceptance are independently verified.

Source framing: [NIST SP 800-61r3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) and [NIST CSF 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework). This is a practical learning adaptation, not a reproduction of normative text.
