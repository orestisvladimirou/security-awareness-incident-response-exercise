# Employee microlearning — report suspected vendor impersonation

**TRAINING DRAFT — NOT SENT TO ANY EMPLOYEE.**

If an unexpected vendor or support message asks you to verify credentials, approve a sign-in or grant additional access, **do not use the embedded link**. Look up the vendor through an approved contact directory and tell your internal service desk or security team.

If you already clicked, entered credentials or approved a prompt, report it immediately using your organization's established security channel. Include the approximate time, sender details, device/account and actions taken. Do not share passwords, tokens or personal information in an informal chat. Rapid reporting helps protect everyone; it should never be punished or concealed.

Your organization must provide the real contact channel before this template is used.
