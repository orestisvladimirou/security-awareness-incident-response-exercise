# 04 — Six-inject tabletop facilitator guide

**NOT AN EXERCISE ALREADY DELIVERED.** This is a ready-to-run script for authorized future practice with consenting team members. No real participants, scored responses or effectiveness outcomes exist in v0.1.

## Exercise objectives

1. Distinguish telemetry from an inference and record unknowns.
2. Demonstrate approved ownership of severity, containment and restoration decisions.
3. Coordinate with a supplier using established contacts without assuming their statements are proof.
4. Decide when specialist personal-data and legal review is required.
5. Explain an incident clearly without unwarranted attribution or exfiltration claims.
6. Propose a measured prevention programme and explain how to test its effect.

## Running the exercise

**Duration:** 90 minutes maximum (75 for injects, 15 for structured debrief). Assign a facilitator, observer, hypothetical incident commander and participating functional roles. The facilitator reads a single inject at each scheduled elapsed minute. The players document: facts, hypotheses, decisions, owner/authorization, missing evidence, communication, and next verification. A scripted answer is not scored as real competence.

The six injects are stored in [the machine-readable facilitator schedule](../data/tabletop_injects.csv). Allow 10–15 minutes of conversation per inject. If the team asks a question outside the available evidence, the correct answer is **unknown or request additional evidence**, not a fabricated fact.

## Observation rubric (future use only)

Score each inject across four dimensions from 0–2, with **no scores filled now**:

- **Evidence discipline:** 0 assumes facts; 1 mixed; 2 separates observed/inferred/missing and states the next proof.
- **Decision authority:** 0 no owner; 1 named but unverified; 2 responsible role and approval gate named.
- **Containment and safety:** 0 unsafe or infeasible; 1 partial; 2 proportionate containment with service risk and tests.
- **Communication and improvement:** 0 speculative or no follow-up; 1 partial; 2 clear uncertainty and measurable post-event learning.

Maximum **8 per inject**, **48 overall**. A score is **not** an accreditation metric or proof of real operational capability. Do not report a pass rate until an authorized exercise is delivered and observers record actual responses.

## Debrief prompts

Which assertions lacked support? Who could authorize each change? What would be needed to sign off a privacy decision? How would an external vendor's inadequate logging affect response? What observable evidence would show a training intervention changed behavior?
